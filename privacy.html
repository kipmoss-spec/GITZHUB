Privacy Information

Your privacy matters to KLYX HUB.

1. Introduction

KLYX HUB respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you when using the platform.

2. Browsing and Downloads

You can browse KLYX HUB and download publicly available applications without creating an account. Registration is optional for ordinary visitors.

3. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we may process information such as:

  • Your email address.
  • Your display name or profile information.
  • Your account identification and creation date.
  • Information you submit when using account features.

We may also collect limited technical information (such as device type or app version) to ensure compatibility and improve the platform.

We do not collect your messages, photos, contacts, or passwords from other applications.

4. How Information Is Used

Account information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your login.
  • Display your profile.
  • Provide account-related features.
  • Improve the platform and fix issues.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored using appropriate security measures. Data transmitted through the platform is protected using HTTPS encryption where supported. Passwords are handled by secure authentication services and are not stored by KLYX HUB in plain text.

Access to account information is restricted to authorized access. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

KLYX HUB does not sell your personal account information.

We only share data with technical service providers as necessary to operate the platform, or if required by law.

If you choose to use a third-party login option, that provider's privacy policy will also apply.

7. Your Choices and Account

You can access and update your available profile information through the My Profile page.

If you want to request correction or deletion of your account information, contact us at privacy@klyxhub.com or through the official support channel.

Direct self-service account deletion is not currently available. Upon a valid deletion request, your personal data will be removed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

8. Changes to This Information

This privacy information may be updated as KLYX HUB develops new features or changes how information is handled. Updates will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at:

privacy@klyxhub.com

Or through the official support channel published on the platform.
